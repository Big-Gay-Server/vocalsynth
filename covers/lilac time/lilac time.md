  "I want to be smiling like myself at any time"

  Aaaand she's done! Boy this has taken muuuch longer than I had originally anticipated, but here she is! Originally I planned to release her sometime in January or February, but I got sick and lost my voice for a good chunk of it and couldn't record until March or so. Since then it's just been cleaning her up and preparing her for release! I did end up cutting the pitches down to 4 instead of 5, my voice just wasn't able to hit as high of a note as it was before unfortunately... I also didn't want to keep the release waiting any longer when I had pretty much everything else done! Maybe in the future I can release an update with the higher pitch :)
  The appends will all be released separately this time around instead of waiting until they were all complete like I did with her v1. Doing that caused a lot of stress on myself, and it kept the voicebank out of your hands as well. Doing the append releases separately should take some of that weight off of my shoulders!

  I hope everyone likes this bank - I worked really hard to make it more accessible and useful than the last! CVVC banks are really an acquired taste, and I understand a lot of people prefer simpler banks like CV or VCV, so I hope that this amalgamation of all of the above suits everyone's needs! Her higher pitches are also hopefully a bit more solid than v1, where I almost had falsetto for the top pitches! This version of the bank meets the plans I had for this bank much better C:

  And with that, please enjoy Merisdae!
