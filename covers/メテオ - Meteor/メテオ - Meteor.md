  Today is Merisdae's 10th anniversary! 10 years ago today, I put my first UTAU cover on youtube - a "Toeto" cover with my UTAU Tsuki Hikari. I recorded her first bank on my laptop microphone, my accent was horrid, and she didn't have an oto, but I put her out into the world 10 years ago today.
  I decided to make this anniversary video a cover of John's "Meteor" using all of my old voicebanks leading up to Merisdae today. Starting with re-otoing and actually tuning my first voicebank, something I didn't do back then ahahaha
  To be completely honest, if you told me in 2010 that I would still be using this silly singing robots program in 10 years, I would have been skeptical (but ultimately I probably would have believed you because using UTAU and being involved in the community has been one of the best things to happen to me!!!!)
  10 years. 10 years!!! That's an entire DECADE of my life that I've dedicated to UTAU, and I wouldn't change it for the world. I've made so many friends, been involved in projects I only thought I would ever aspire to be in, and it really has changed my life for the better. I don't know where I would be right now had I not recorded Tsuki's first crappy CV bank 10 years ago!
