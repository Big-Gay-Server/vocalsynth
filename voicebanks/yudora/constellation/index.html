Yudora -CONSTELLATION-

Thank you for your interest in Yudora's -CONSTELLATION- Voicebank Set!

-CONSTELLATION- is a collection of 3 voicebanks (starcore, nebula, and supernova) with a variety of expressions ranging from soft and sweet to solid and powerful. These voicebanks were designed for use in OpenUTAU but have been made backwards compatible to Classic UTAU as well!

For instructions on how to install Yudora -CONSTELLATION- voicebanks, please see below!

How to install:

OpenUTAU

  1. Click the download button with the OpenUTAU logo, and download the desired voicebank .zip file from Google Drive.
  2. Once downloaded, you can simply select "Install Singer..." from the "Tools" menu within OpenUTAU.
  3. From there, you can select the voicebank .zip file, and OpenUTAU will begin to import.
  4. Archive File Encoding and Text File Encoding should both be set to "Japanese (Shift-JIS)". Then, click "Install". Done!

UTAU

  1. Click the download button with the UTAU logo, and download the desired voicebank .zip file from Google Drive.
  2. Once downloaded, you can simply drag and drop the voicebank .zip file into UTAU.
  3. UTAU will confirm you would like to install the voicebank. Click "OK", and UTAU will install the voicebank. Done!


Key Art: GoodnightAya


-starcore-

a stable and consistent tone, with clear enunciation and good pitch accuracy.
suitable for a wide range of musical genres, from pop to rock to ballads.



japanese (CVVC)
Pitches: A3, D4, G4



-supernova-

a strong and forceful tone, with a lot of energy and intensity. It is ideal for genres
such as rock, metal, and electronic music that require power and presence.



japanese (CVVC)
Pitches: A3, D4, G4



-nebula-

a gentle and airy tone, with a lot of breathiness and vulnerability. It is ideal for
ballads and slow-tempo songs that require a more delicate sound.



japanese (CVVC)
Pitches: A3, D4, G4
